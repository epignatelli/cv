\noindent\textit{Re: RL Fellows Program}
\vspace{2em}

\noindent To whom it may concern at \textit{Anthropic},
\vspace{1em}

\noindent
I am writing to apply for the \emph{RL Fellows Program} at \textbf{Anthropic}.
My research sits at the intersection of deep RL and large language models, with a focus on \emph{credit assignment} and \emph{scalable RL environments}.
I combine an academic track record in RL/LLMs with five years of industry experience shipping ML systems, and I am motivated by the challenge of \emph{scaling RL} to produce safe, capable AI.

\paragraph{Academic and research experience.}
I am an \emph{Assistant Professor in Machine Learning at UCL} and \emph{Associate Faculty} at the \link{https://bold-lab.ai/}{BOLD Lab} — a £60M UKRI-funded national AI research lab (Oxford, UCL, Imperial) led by Jakob Foerster, focused on open-ended learning and embodied AI.
I lead three modules, teach in several others, and have been nominated for the \emph{UCL Inspiring Teaching Delivery Award} two years running.

My \emph{PhD} at UCL, with Laura Toni and Tim Rocktäschel, focused on the Credit Assignment Problem at the intersection of deep RL and LLMs.
I authored a \emph{TMLR Survey Certification}-awarded survey on temporal credit assignment, showed that LLMs align with human evaluations of action relevance in RL, built \emph{NAVIX} — a widely adopted JAX reimplementation of MiniGrid at thousands of parallel environments per GPU — and co-authored \emph{BALROG} (ICLR 2025), a benchmark for LLM/VLM agents on long-horizon tasks.

\paragraph{Research agenda.}
My current work develops \emph{latent option models} that scale options — temporally extended actions — by learning a reusable vocabulary of latent actions from large-scale, \emph{action-free} demonstrations, which can then be fine-tuned with RLHF/RLAIF.
This connects directly to the Fellowship's workstreams: multi-turn reasoning and long-horizon tool use in Claude require RL signals that propagate meaningfully across hundreds of steps — the harder the credit assignment problem, the more principled the training scheme needs to be.

\paragraph{Industry experience.}
Before academia, I spent \emph{five years} translating ML research into commercial products.
As \emph{ML Lead} at Burohappold Engineering (2017–2020, London), I delivered deep-learning tools now used by the \emph{Premier League}, creating new revenue streams.
At Gridshell.it, open-source genetic algorithms for structural engineering received the \emph{Peter Lord Award}.
These roles gave me a \emph{systems mindset} — reproducible codebases and infrastructure in JAX/PyTorch for long-running experiments — and the ability to align scientists, engineers, and stakeholders around ambitious deliverables.

\paragraph{Why the RL Fellows Program at Anthropic?}
Anthropic is doing the most \emph{ambitious RL-for-alignment} work in the field, at a scale no academic group can match.
The Fellowship offers direct mentorship from researchers defining the frontier of post-training, the compute to carry out the experiments for \emph{latent option models} that would compress a year of progress into four months, and a \emph{clear path} to contributing to Anthropic's mission long-term and full-time.
My agenda — principled environment design and temporally extended action learning — aligns closely with the goal of improving Claude's capabilities through RL, and I look forward to discussing how I can contribute.
